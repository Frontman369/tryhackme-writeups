# Wireshark: The Basics - TryHackMe Writeup

## Overview
This room introduced the fundamentals of Wireshark and basic packet analysis. It focused on understanding how network traffic can be captured, inspected, and filtered to investigate communication between systems.

---

## Objectives
- Learning how to navigate and configure Wireshark  
- Inspecting packets across different TCP/IP layers  
- Applying display filters to analyze traffic efficiently  
- Understanding the basics of packet capture (PCAP) analysis  

---

## Tools & Concepts Used
- Wireshark  
- Packet analysis  
- TCP/IP protocol inspection  
- Display filtering  
- PCAP investigation  

---

## Key Concepts

### Packet Inspection
I learned how Wireshark captures and displays network packets, allowing detailed inspection of transmitted data across different protocol layers.

---

### TCP/IP Layer Analysis
The room demonstrated how packet information is organized within the TCP/IP stack and how different protocols can be analyzed individually.

---

### Filtering Traffic
I used display filters to isolate specific packets and protocols, making traffic analysis more efficient and manageable.

---

### Navigation & Packet Dissection
I explored the Wireshark interface, packet structure, and how protocol details are broken down during packet dissection.

---

## Challenges Faced
- Understanding packet structures across multiple layers  
- Navigating large amounts of captured traffic  
- Learning how filtering improves investigation efficiency  

---

## Key Takeaways
- Wireshark is a powerful network traffic analysis tool  
- Packet inspection helps understand how systems communicate  
- Filtering is essential for efficient traffic investigation  
- Understanding protocols becomes easier through packet-level visibility  

---

## Reflection
This room provided my first practical introduction to network traffic analysis using Wireshark. It helped me understand how communication appears at the packet level and how analysts investigate captured traffic in cybersecurity environments.

The room also reinforced previous networking concepts by allowing me to observe protocols directly within packet captures.
